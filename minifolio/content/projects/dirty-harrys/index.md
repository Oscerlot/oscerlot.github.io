>  Rip up the streets of Neon City as a One-Car Thunderstorm. Your mission is to cause as much destruction as possible on the city's busiest highway. Your weapons? Wrecking Balls, Dynamite, and the pure speed of your prized Red Stallion. 

- Ultimately, this is game that a small team of us made during the 2020 Global Game Jam, built across 3 days, inspired by the Takedown Mode from Burnout 3. 
- We enjoyed it a lot, and so did everyone who played it, so we released it on Steam for free as an experiment to see what would happen.

So for some fun stats:
- To date, it's had:
  - 24.6 million store impressions
  - 456,000 page views
  - 57,522 downloads
  - 661 wishlists (I still don't get why someone wishlists a free game)

but 90% of all that activity occured within the first 4 months of release.
