## What's the IP/Game?
- Gabby's Dollhouse is **#1 most-watched animated series on Netflix**, and has a vast range of toys
- Mighty Kingdom developed the accompanying mobile game, which has achieved **over 20m installs** over its lifetime. 
- The premise of the app follows the same as the show: to explore the dollhouse, create new things, and play with friends! 
- While it can be played independantly, the game does integrate with the physical toys, where players can scan a QR code that comes with their toy to then add it into their game to play with. 

## So what did I actually do?
- I took over as **Game Lead** in 2024, several years into the product's life, **managing the team and direction** as part of ongoing game updates. 

- This included a major update alongside the release of the Gabby's Dollhouse Movie in 2025, which introduced new content, minigames, toy integrations, and long-standing bug fixes to improve the overall playability of the game. 

- Overall, my responsabilities were:
  - Directing design on updates to give the best player experience
  - Ongoing comms with the IP holder
  - Team planning and resourcing
  - Release management
  - General in-engine work
  - Occasional QA
