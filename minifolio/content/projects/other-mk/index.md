A non-exhaustive list I'll be adding to over time of other projects that I was involved with at Mighty Kingdom.

## Conan Chop Chop

<div style="background-image: url('/microfolio/content/projects/other-mk/images/conan-chop-chop.jpg'); height: 250px; background-size: cover;"></div>

>Conan Chop Chop is a party roguelite for 1-4 players full of mayhem, loot, and adventure. In this outrageous take on Conan the Barbarian, you are the muscle-bound hero foolish enough to try and stop the evil wizard Thoth-Amon! 
- Released on Steam, PS4, Xbox One, and Switch in 2022. 
- I was brought in occasionally to consult on design feedback, progression, and the onboarding experience.
- Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1061880/Conan_Chop_Chop/

## Serenity's Spa

<div style="background-image: url('/microfolio/content/projects/other-mk/images/spa-image.png'); height: 300px; background-size: cover;"></div>

- A tycoon-management game for mobile, where players run a beauty salon, and serve customers.
- Originally commisioned by Lion Studios and developed by Mighty Kingdom, it was released in 2024 with over 1m+ downloads.
- I served as the **Release Manager** on the project, including working with the SDK and Ad vendors to ensure a smooth launch.
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.gxgames.spa&hl=en_AU
