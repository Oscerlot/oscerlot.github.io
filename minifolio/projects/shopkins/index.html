Shopkins Portfolio

Portfolio of games for the Shopkins IP by Moose

There were three Shopkins games I primarily worked on during my time at Mighty Kingdom - World Vacation, Cutie Cars, and World, with World Vacation and Cutie Cars being the first and second projects I touched at the studio.

Shopkins: World Vacation (2017 - 2019)

Travel the world with your favorite Shoppies, and meet brand new Shopkins along the way. First stop is Europe with tons of fun and challenging bubble-popping puzzles to be found in the UK, France and Italy!

  • I started just after the onset of development as my first project at Mighty Kingdom.
  • My role covered all aspects of design, in particular the level design, and technical design of the unique bubble types.
  • Occasionally it would include creating 2D Art for bubble types.
  • I eventually took over as Game Lead in 2019, continuing with content updates while we developed Wild Life: Puzzle Story with the same tech.

Shopkins: Cutie Cars (2017 - 2020)

It’s life in the express lane! Join Cupcake Cruiser, Choc Chip Racer, Motor Melon, Donut Express and all their friends on their wheelie fun rides. With 20 Cutie Cars to collect, and more coming soon, the fun doesn’t take a brake in Shopkins: Cutie Cars! We <3 QT Cars!

  • A Pacman-256 style endless runner where players navigate their Cutie Car through the streets. Collect puzzle pieces to unlock new Cutie Cars with special abilities!
  • My role was primarily in the level design (level segments that procedually connect), technical design of the car abilities, and the puzzle-piece gacha system and the delivery mechanics of it.

Shopkins World (2017)

Explore Shopville and play cute and fun mini games with all of your favorite Shopkins friends! Help decorate the Shoppies Café, get creative in the Cupcake bake shop, go bowling with the Shopkins, visit the spooky haunted house, help Donatina at her Donut Cart and more! Once you shop, you can't stop! Collect and play with all your favorite Shopkins from Seasons 1 to 10 and add them to your My Shopkins Collection

  • Originally released in 2015, I was involved with the later 2.0 redesign of Shopkins World, which hosted over 20m players globally.
  • Was involved in a design capacity, focusing on user experience improvements, and minigame design.
